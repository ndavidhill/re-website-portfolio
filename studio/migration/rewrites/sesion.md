### Sesión: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How do you change the way a whole country drinks tequila?

By 2020, clear spirits were Australia's fastest-growing alcohol category — but led by vodka and gin. Tequila had one role in the national habit: shots, lots of them, late at night. Sesión — a sipping tequila crafted by Jennifer Hawkins and Jake Wall — needed a brand that didn't just look premium, but actively shifted behaviour: from the last drink of the night to the first.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We designed the brand to reposition tequila from shot to sundowner.

We built Sesión to behave like an early-evening ritual, not a midnight dare. The brand signals a premium tequila experience made to be sipped and savoured — repositioning the category in people's minds and giving them permission to reach for tequila the way they already reach for vodka and gin.

#### Signature Behaviours

### How the brand moves tequila earlier in the night.

### Sunset escape

The O in the logo dips over the horizon like a setting sun, and photography brings the long shadows and muted tones of evening — the brand behaves like golden hour, cueing the first drink, not the last.

### Set up to steal share

By positioning tequila to be enjoyed across the evening like vodka — the biggest category by volume and value — the brand sets Sesión up to take share from a thriving segment, not just sell another bottle.

#### The Proof

### A premium tequila built to change a habit, not just enter a category.

Sesión behaves as an invitation to drink tequila differently — slow, premium, early — repositioning the spirit to compete with Australia's favourite drinks rather than its end-of-night ones.
