### True North: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How do you convince high-end travellers to leave their comfort zone — when luxury usually means a beach chair?

By 2021, everyone needed a holiday, and nothing said luxury like a beachside resort. True North sells the opposite: adventure cruise travel through Australia, Papua New Guinea and Indonesia, designed to push a small group of guests out of their comfort zones. The brand had to behave like a premium invitation to the unknown — refined enough to reassure luxury travellers, wild enough to promise genuine discovery.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We designed the brand to behave as a guide to new frontiers.

We built True North to behave like a compass pointing somewhere worth going. It takes guests on a journey of discovery — away to remote lands and back to themselves — nodding to its nautical heritage while signalling a premium edge for travellers who've lived virtually for far too long.

#### Signature Behaviours

### How the brand balances refined and wild.

### A modern marine compass

The logo reimagines the marine compass, emphasising True North's role in guiding people through their journey and tying neatly to the name — the brand behaves like navigation, not decoration.

### Refined yet wild

The identity nods to a time before screens and Google Earth, reminding guests of real exploration — premium in craft, adventurous in spirit, primed to deliver as international travel reopens.

#### The Proof

### A premium brand built to behave like an adventure, not a resort.

As the world emerged from lockdown, True North stood ready to deliver adventure with a premium edge — a story of new frontiers built to connect with travellers hungry for the real thing after too long lived virtually.
