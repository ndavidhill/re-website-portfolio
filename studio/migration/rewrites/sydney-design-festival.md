### Sydney Design Festival: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How does a design festival behave like design is for everyone — not just the inner-city few?

Design has a reputation for being understood by a privileged few — and design businesses are sometimes part of the problem. The 2018 Sydney Design Festival, curated by MAAS, set out to challenge that with the theme of accessing design. The brief: stop preaching to the people who already attend, and behave in a way that broadens design across geography, race, gender and socioeconomics.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We designed an identity that behaves as many things to many people.

We built a campaign identity with no single "right way" to define design. Drawing on MAAS's vast collection — toys to technology, furniture to fashion — the logo evolves endlessly to communicate the breadth of what design is, who it's for and how it makes people feel. The brand behaves as inclusively as the idea it's arguing for.

#### Signature Behaviours

### How an evolving logo opens design up.

### A logo that's never the same twice

Endless iterations show design as many different things, relevant to many different people — the identity behaves like the diversity it's championing rather than a fixed mark.

### Design everywhere, on everything

Because great design lives in everyday objects, the campaign appeared on t-shirts, tote bags and more — inclusive, a little irreverent, positioning design as something anyone can experience and enjoy.

### Festivals within the festival

The evolving logo created mini-festivals for specific audiences — Sydney After Dark for busy workers, Sydney Ankle Biter for families in the outer suburbs — so the brand behaves differently for the people it's trying to reach.

#### The Proof

### A festival that behaved like an invitation, not a gate.

By making the identity as broad and irreverent as the city it served, the festival repositioned design as something for absolutely everyone — extending its reach well beyond the inner-city set that always shows up.
