### CommBank: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How does Australia's biggest bank behave like it has actually changed — not just say it has?

After the financial crisis, a reputation reckoning, devastating bushfires and a pandemic, faith in institutions was thin. CommBank's black-and-yellow diamond — unchanged since 1991 — had come to signal exactly the kind of authority Australians had stopped trusting. The brand couldn't announce change with a campaign. It had to behave differently in every interaction, or the change wouldn't be believed.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We turned a corporate sign-off into a beacon.

We rebuilt the diamond to behave, not just sit. Reconstructed as a single built object — lit from within, casting a real shadow — it stopped acting like an institutional stamp at the end of an ad and started behaving like a beacon that leads. The whole system was designed to move with that same lighter, more open intent.

#### Signature Behaviours

### Three ways the brand acts more open than it used to.

### The diamond leads, not signs off

The beacon that guides the way forward. It has become a symbol of positivity for the future, one that represents a new type of leadership. In application, the mark is freer, behaving less like a traditional sign-off and more like a light that points to what's next.

### Design that earns its place

Simple shapes and strokes direct the eye and carry a sense of motion — nothing sits there for its own sake. A lighter, more energetic system is complemented by a brighter palette alongside the heritage yellow and black, so the brand communicates faster and warmer in the moments that matter.

### Behave consistently where money moves

The principle of simplicity carries into product. The Neo card heroes the brighter yellow to signal a refreshed credit offering; StepPay — CommBank's first buy-now-pay-later product — reimagines the brand's graphic shapes in soft digital gradients. The change shows up where customers actually transact, not just in advertising.

#### Signature Moment: Light as a material

### Where the brand stops being printed and starts being built.

In environment, the diamond's logic becomes physical — actual light brightens and directs the space, with tonal greys and timber bringing warmth. The brand isn't applied to the wall; it's built into the room.

#### The Proof

### A 30-year-old icon, rebuilt to lead.

For the first time in almost three decades, CommBank changed its identity — not to look new, but to behave like a different kind of bank: lighter, humbler, more open. The diamond now works as a beacon for what the organisation wants to become, carrying a new strategy and values into products, spaces and partnerships.
