### Musson: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How does a 50-year cult jeweller behave like a coveted luxury brand — at every touchpoint, not just in the case?

Founded on Sydney's North Shore, Musson Jewellery had been a collectors' name for over half a century. As a new generation of the family stepped up, the ambition was to carry the brand's famed obsession with quality into every part of the customer experience — moving from cult favourite to coveted Australian luxury. Craftsmanship in the product wasn't enough; the brand had to behave with the same craft everywhere.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We designed the brand to behave with the craft of the jewellery itself.

We took the detail and beauty of Musson's work as the brief for the brand. A bold, rich pattern and confident modern shapes — drawn from the monogram — let the identity build on heritage while moving into a contemporary world, so the brand carries the same craft as the pieces.

#### Signature Behaviours

### How craft shows up beyond the product.

### Connecting past and future

The identity system draws its pattern and forms from the monogram and the jewellery's detail — honouring 50 years of heritage while behaving like a brand built for now.

### Intimate, nostalgic art direction

Photography elevates simple, everyday moments into something precious — confident in approach, intimate in feel — so the brand captures the quiet luxury of the work rather than shouting it.

### One Musson experience, anywhere

Experience principles ensure individual care and attention hold true wherever people meet the brand — a refreshed global online journey, from browsing to purchase, supported every step, just like in store.

#### The Proof

### Cult favourite, behaving like Australian luxury.

The new brand lets Musson deliver a true Musson experience for every customer — in the retail store or browsing the latest collection from anywhere in the world — carrying its craftsmanship into every interaction, not just the jewellery box.
