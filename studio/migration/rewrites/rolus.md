### Rolus: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How does a drink behave like a relief — in a category drowning in self-importance?

The world doesn't need another self-important drink. Among the "braincare beverages" and life-changing alcohol alternatives sits Rolus: a sparkling drink made to hydrate. That's it. In a space full of soft gradients, hyper-bravado and pseudo-science, the brand's job wasn't to out-promise the category — it was to behave like the opposite of it: bold, self-assured, and refreshingly free of nonsense.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We designed the brand to behave like it has nothing to prove.

Rolus doesn't overcomplicate and doesn't overthink — so neither does its brand. A pared-back palette, a confident unadorned graphic system and bold typography let it stand out by stripping back. It's for the never still: refreshing after yoga, over brunch, on a boat, or mixed with your spirit of choice.

#### Signature Behaviours

### How restraint becomes the loudest thing in the room.

### A voice that gets to the point

The tone is unapologetic, uncomplicated and self-aware — happy to call out industry nonsense and bring moments in time to life, from morning yoga to happy hour. The brand behaves like a friend who doesn't oversell.

### Digital-first, story-rich

Sold mostly direct to consumers in the US, the brand is built digital-first without losing the narrative — every click, scroll and rollover blends product discovery with story.

### A brand that changes with the day

Made to go from day to night, the messaging shifts dynamically across 24 hours — the brand literally behaves differently depending on when you meet it.

#### The Proof

### A challenger that wins by refusing to play the category's game.

By behaving with confidence and clarity in a space defined by bravado and pseudo-science, Rolus carves out distinctiveness the hard way — a hydration drink that stands for less, and stands out because of it.
