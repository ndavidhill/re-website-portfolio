### NZR+: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How does New Zealand rugby behave like it's in the room with fans on the other side of the world?

In New Zealand, rugby isn't a game — it's a way of life, stitched into the culture. But that closeness is hard to feel from afar. New Zealand Rugby's new streaming platform, NZR+, had to behave as a direct line to the heart of the sport: exclusive behind-the-scenes content, interviews, classic matches and originals that make a global fan feel as close as a local.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We designed the platform to behave with deep respect for the country, the players and the fans.

NZR+ was built to carry the spirit of New Zealand rugby to the world. Black sits front and centre, the craft honours the players, and the experience is made to feel like belonging — for the diehards and the once-every-World-Cup crowd alike.

#### Signature Behaviours

### How the brand stays unmistakably Kiwi.

### Drawn from New Zealand itself

Black is highlighted with greens and purples from the Pāua shell unique to the country — and the logo hides the famous fern, with a cheeky kiwi tucked into the "R". The brand behaves like where it's from.

### Made for every kind of fan

The experience captures more than big hits and big wins — it carries the passion behind the scenes, the highs and lows, and a distinctly Kiwi sense of humour, so any fan finds their way in.

#### The Proof

### A platform that brought a million fans closer.

Since launching in August 2023, the All Blacks YouTube channel surged 79% — from 560,000 subscribers to 1 million by December 2024, a gain of 440,000. As NZR's Head of Brand put it: the platform is "made for fans… an opportunity to feel closer to the game than ever before."
