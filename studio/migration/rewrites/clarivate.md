### Clarivate: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How does an $11B analytics leader behave like it exists — when nobody knows its name?

Clarivate's specialist businesses — CompuMark, Web of Science — were respected in their fields, but the masterbrand behind them was invisible: the biggest company most people had never heard of. In a pandemic that made innovation everyone's priority, a fragmented house of products couldn't tell a connected story. The brand had to behave as a single front door to the whole innovation lifecycle, not a holding company customers never met.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We designed the brand to bring human ingenuity into focus.

We pivoted Clarivate to behave as one masterbrand — the single entry point to market-leading analytics across research, protection and commercialisation. Built on the idea of optics, a lens derived from the logo brings precision and clarity to everything, giving each specialist business its own world while unifying them under one clear system.

#### Signature Behaviours

### How a quiet giant learned to act like a leader.

### One lens, many worlds

The Clarivate lens gives each business a distinct brand world — CompuMark's trademark protection, Web of Science's connected research — while a shared clean aesthetic and Clarivate purple hold them together. The portfolio finally behaves as a connected whole, not a set of strangers.

### A connected digital home

A fragmented presence across 16 misaligned websites was consolidated into a single digital home — one grid, one typography, one content and UX approach — with a component library that lets Clarivate's team build high-quality experiences fast and cheap.

### Tell the story like a human

An emotive brand film reorientates a complex business around customer benefit — using a goldfish as a warm storytelling device to carry a brilliant idea through every stage of innovation to a life-changing drug. The brand explains itself in human terms, which was disruptive for the category.

#### Signature Moment: Saying hello to the world

### Times Square and the Stock Exchange, in constant motion.

To introduce the quiet giant after its NYSE listing, a "flipping discs" system took over two of the world's most iconic spaces — discs turning to reveal each flagship business colour, capturing a business of living data and energy in perpetual motion.

#### The Proof

### A masterbrand built to carry the business into new growth.

Clarivate now behaves as one confident brand across print, film, motion, language, events and environments. Even against the backdrop of a global pandemic the business grew revenue and profit, lifted employee engagement, and moved into a major strategic merger — a once-invisible giant, finally in focus.
