### Optus: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How does a telco behave like a premium network experience — when SIMs are sold at supermarkets?

By 2020, streaming was second nature, 5G had arrived, and even supermarkets were selling SIMs. For Optus, the category was louder, faster, and more complex — so the brand had to do more than explain plans. It had to feel like connection: energetic, confident, human, and premium — at scale.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We rebuilt "Yes" as an engine — not a slogan.

We centred the system on Optus' iconic "Yes" and rebuilt the brand to behave with more energy and intent across every touchpoint. The goal wasn't a new look — it was a clearer, more consistent experience of the brand in motion.

#### Signature Behaviours

### Three rules for how the brand acts at every scale.

### Behave with energy

"Yes" becomes the engine of a more energetic system — expressing dynamism and wit, not corporate telco polish.

### Behave with confident clarity

Colour simplified; typography used more boldly — so the brand communicates faster and cleaner in the moments that matter.

### Behave at retail scale

The system was designed to live in physical space, not just in comms — showing the true scale of Optus, not just what they sell.

#### Signature Moment: Retail Reimagined

### Where the brand stops selling and starts connecting.

In the flagship Brisbane store, the identity comes to life through immersive idents inspired by the Australian environment — turning retail into a felt experience of connectedness and energy, not a showroom of devices.

#### The Proof

### The strongest brand in Australia — built to behave, not just appear.

Optus was named the strongest brand in Australia by Brand Finance. The system was built to be highly practical while still expressive — supporting a complex, evolving organisation at scale.
