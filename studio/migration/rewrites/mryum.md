### Mr Yum: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How does a QR-code platform behave like it's human — not just functional?

If you've eaten out lately, you've scanned a Mr Yum code. The mobile ordering and payments service had become critical infrastructure for venues — but infrastructure rarely feels like anything. To carry a new "Better Together" positioning across every part of the business, and capture the energy of its culture, the brand had to behave like the people and food it sits between, not the tech underneath.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We designed the brand to behave as a fusion of food, tech and people.

We built a system that holds both sides of Mr Yum at once: the precision of a product and the warmth of a host. Tech-inspired structure meets genuine personality, so "Better Together" isn't a tagline — it's how the brand acts across product, marketing and culture.

#### Signature Behaviours

### How structure and personality coexist.

### Tech you can feel

A visible grid underpins every layout and a custom typeface by Wei Huang nods to QR codes (interchangeable with Söhne) — the engineering shows through, deliberately, so the brand reads as a product-first business.

### Personality on the plate

A cute iconography suite and flavour-filled secondary colours warm up the black-and-white palette, giving the brand the energy of the venues and team it serves.

### Behave product-first

The ordering experience stays simple — a dynamic digital frame carrying the seamless move from device to table — while the website lets the brand get expressive for partners, balancing utility with fun.

#### The Proof

### A platform that now behaves with as much energy as the rooms it's in.

The rebrand gave the team a system with no creative ceiling — "a total game-changer," in one designer's words, taking the work "from entrée-sized options to a full banquet" — and created a buzz that carried from product into the brand's events and culture.
