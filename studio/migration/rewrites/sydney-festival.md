### Sydney Festival: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How do you celebrate a city that means something different to everyone?

By 2021, Sydney had taken a battering — a pandemic on top of a decade of lockout laws and noise complaints that had dimmed its nightlife. But coming out of lockdown, Sydneysiders were ready to reconnect. Sydney Festival had a chance to capture the essence of a city that means something different to all of us — which meant the brand couldn't behave like one fixed version of Sydney. It had to hold every version at once.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We designed the brand to behave as everyone's Sydney, not one Sydney.

The answer was "Find your Sydney side" — an idea that celebrates every side of the real Sydney while reflecting the array of festival experiences on offer. No Harbour Bridge-and-Opera-House cliché; a logo and design system as big, bold and unapologetic as the city, built so each person could see themselves in it.

#### Signature Behaviours

### How the brand made room for every Sydney.

### Find your Sydney side

The identity spans the whole city, celebrating its many sides — a system flexible enough that everyone finds their own Sydney in it, brought to life as boldly as the place itself.

### Take it to the streets

OOH from Circular Quay to Parramatta invited audiences to identify with their own Sydney side and explore what the festival offered — celebrating every side of the city at broad scale.

### Sides as festival experiences

The different Sydney sides weren't just visual — textures and themes were connected to specific events, turning the idea into personalised experiences for festivalgoers.

#### Signature Moment: A TVC made by the village

### When lockdown limits became the creative.

With a fresh lockdown limiting production, the TVC came together with Vandal and Sydney Festival chipping in homemade footage — a favourite Redfern pub stepping up as the venue. The brand behaved like the community it was celebrating.

#### The Proof

### A city that turned out — in record numbers.

Over 300,000 attendees (up 88% on 2021), more than 141,000 ticketed (up 127%), website users up 47%, and a 97.4% average TVC view-through rate. As festival director Olivia Ansell put it: "the soul of a Sydneysider never disappears… I really wanted this new brand to capture that."
