### Skyscanner: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How does a fast-moving global platform behave coherently — as it enters markets that have never seen it?

Since 2001, Skyscanner's travel metasearch had grown exponentially across the UK, Europe and Australia. With a new strategy and fresh markets in its sights, the brand risked behaving inconsistently at the exact moment first impressions mattered most. It needed coherent, brand-led experiences that could cut through in new markets while deepening connection with the customers it already had.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We embedded as Skyscanner's design partner — and made the brand behave as one system.

Rather than hand over a guidelines PDF, we became a single integrated design team with Skyscanner's people. We immersed in the new strategy and existing brand, then evolved and amplified it for cut-through — so the brand behaves coherently wherever it shows up, in any market.

#### Signature Behaviours

### How a global brand learned to act with one voice.

### Optimise, then amplify

We removed what was no longer needed, introduced new colour, explored motion signatures and built photography principles — keeping the brand locally relevant and globally consistent, then embedding it through digital guidelines teams actually use.

### One integrated design team

Working in Figma and Slack, we operated as a single team with Skyscanner — sharing thinking and prototypes seamlessly to produce solutions and principles now adopted across all brand experiences.

#### The Proof

### A more confident, cohesive brand — built to travel.

The work produced a more coherent brand system and a series of digital campaigns that built recognition and connected with customers across the UK, Europe, North America and Asia — with the design principles now standard across Skyscanner's experiences.
