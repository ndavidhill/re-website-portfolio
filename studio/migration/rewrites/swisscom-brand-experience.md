### Swisscom: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How does a trusted national icon behave like it's still ahead — without spending its trust?

Swisscom is Switzerland's leading telecoms and one of its leading IT companies, serving an enormous breadth of people — mobile, broadband, pay-TV, SME and enterprise. But a decade-old identity was showing its age, and market leaders can't stand still. The brand had to behave more coherently across every place people met it, and feel like the most trusted Swiss tech innovator — without trading away the trust it had earned.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We designed the brand to behave as a guiding light.

Across every audience, Swisscom plays one enduring role: a guide and companion in a changing world. We turned that into a behaviour we called the Guiding Light — as unique and variable as the Alpine glow — that doesn't just decorate the brand, it helps customers move through it.

#### Signature Behaviours

### How light became a functional behaviour, not a graphic.

### Light that guides

As a digital behaviour, the glow is used functionally — highlighting and focusing as customers interact with products and services. The brand helps at every step, behaving like a hand on the shoulder through the experience.

### Light that moves people

Aesthetically, the same light reveals and excites — building on Swisscom's established trust while elevating the brand to feel contemporary and digitally native, true to a purpose of "empowering the digital future".

### Prototyped in product

The light shaped real prototypes — richer experiences whether searching for products, paying a bill or watching entertainment — and informed motion, art direction, illustration and environments throughout.

#### The Proof

### The world's 3rd strongest brand.

The brand-led experience work helped Swisscom reconnect with residential and commercial customers alike — and in 2023, Swisscom was ranked the world's 3rd strongest brand in Brand Finance's Global 500.
