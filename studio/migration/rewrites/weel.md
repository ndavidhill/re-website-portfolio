### Weel: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How do you make expenses behave like everyone's responsibility — not everyone's chore?

By 2022, nearly half of CFOs thought their expense processes worked fine. Almost every employee hated doing expenses. Born from the misery of sharing the office's one corporate card, Weel (formerly DiviPay) set out to democratise spend management. The brand had to behave for two audiences at once: reassure the CFO, delight the employee — and position the product as a global-leading solution.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We designed the brand around collective accountability.

We gave Weel a strategic purpose — transform businesses through collective accountability — on the belief that when everybody is valued, everybody adds value. Translated into the tagline "Everybody wins", the brand behaves as a shared system rather than a finance-team gatekeeper.

#### Signature Behaviours

### How the brand turns a chore into shared ownership.

### A name on a forward trajectory

A reimagining of "wheel", Weel captures momentum and growth — a distinctive, ownable name built to stand out and expand across Australian and overseas markets.

### A system built from the card

Like a wheel of many spokes, Weel is many features around one core: the virtual corporate card. A 3D wheel-logomark made of cards inspired the whole system — a holding device for type and photography, a cropped logomark, and a card device that brings part of an image into focus.

### Every expense tells a story

Rather than stock illustration, customer-centred photography and motion show collective accountability in action — businesses thriving — injecting warmth into an edgy, zingy brand and acknowledging every expense is made with intent.

#### Signature Moment: Weel in motion

### Cards that fold, spin and click into place.

The website is a kit of parts built on card shapes — animated cards folding in and out, hovering and clicking into place — and in slow motion the card becomes an abstract, accordion-like creature, reinforcing constant momentum.

#### The Proof

### A spend platform that behaves like everybody's in it together.

Weel emerged with a crystallised purpose, sharpened messaging and a distinctive new system — "a seamless experience and a great collaboration," in its CEO's words — positioning a once-internal tool as a global-leading spend management brand.
