### Domain: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How does a listings portal behave like a place of possibility — not a transaction?

By 2021, homes had quietly become everything — office, classroom, sanctuary, dance floor. But property brands still behaved like transaction engines: list, inspect, sold. Domain risked being remembered only for the moment money changed hands. To matter beyond the transaction, the brand had to behave like a guide to what a home could be, not a database of what's for sale.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We designed the brand to open possibilities, not close deals.

We repositioned Domain to behave as a place people go to discover new possibilities for themselves — and rebuilt the system, from strategy to advertising to the employee experience, around that single behaviour. Every touchpoint was designed to shine a light forward rather than chase the next sold sign.

#### Signature Behaviours

### Two systems, one behaviour — inside and out.

### Behave like a guide, not a portal

The refreshed brand and campaign present Domain in a new light — not a real estate company looking for the next sold sign, but a property portal continuously improving Australian lives by shining a light on possibilities. It moves Domain in people's minds from transactional listings site to a place of discovery.

### An employer brand that unifies

A deep immersion defined what makes working at Domain special — beyond culture and a challenger mindset, it was the drive to build a category-defining property marketplace that unites the whole business. The resulting EVP creates cohesion that feels both familiar and fresh, shaping the Group's people experience for years to come.

#### The Proof

### A property brand that behaves like it's on your side.

Domain shifted from transactional portal to a brand about possibility — aligned across strategy, advertising and its people. The EVP now shapes the Group's people experience for years to come, with leadership describing Re as a true partner that challenged them to be aspirational while staying true to who they are.
