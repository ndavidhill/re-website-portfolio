### MA Financial Group: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How does a finance firm behave like an innovation company — in a category no one calls innovative?

By 2021, banking and finance rarely made anyone's list of Australia's most innovative industries. Yet MA Financial Group — ASX-listed, spanning asset management, corporate advisory and equities — thrived precisely on visionary thinking, cracking problems others couldn't (the Significant Investor Visa among them). After a decade of growth, the brand needed to behave like what the business already was: an innovator that connects the dots others miss.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We designed the brand to bring ideas into the light.

We rebuilt the brand around MA Financial Group's real talent — uncovering opportunities others overlook through the cross-pollination of data, people and businesses. Light became the behaviour: it passes across the monogram to reveal the initials, and a striking Australian light runs through the photography and palette. The brand acts like clarity arriving.

#### Signature Behaviours

### How the brand makes invisible thinking visible.

### Light reveals the idea

The monogram doesn't just sit there — light moves across it to uncover the initials, behaving as a small act of revelation that mirrors how the firm surfaces opportunities others can't see.

### An unmistakably Australian light

A distinctive Australian light pervades the photography and colour, grounding a global-minded firm in where it's from and giving the brand a warmth the category rarely allows itself.

#### The Proof

### An Australian innovation company, finally behaving like one.

The brand now positions MA Financial Group as an innovation company that can go anywhere and achieve anything on the clarity of its ideas — reflecting the full scope of the business and the cross-pollination that drives it.
