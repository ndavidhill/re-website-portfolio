### Maurice Blackburn: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How does a top law firm behave like justice is for everyone — not just those who can afford it?

Maurice Blackburn helped win the 40-hour week and equal pay for women. But the legal system still behaves like it's built for insiders, leaving most people shut out at the moment they need it most. As Australia's leading plaintiff firm, Maurice Blackburn needed a brand that didn't just claim the law should serve everyone — it had to act approachable, inclusive and human at every point a first-timer meets it.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We designed the brand to put the person, not the case, at the centre.

We rebuilt the brand to behave as a supportive expert — one that sees you for more than your legal matter. The enduring red square stopped being a static mark and started behaving as a frame: expanded into layers that hold real clients' photography and words, physically placing people at the centre of Maurice Blackburn's world.

#### Signature Behaviours

### Behaviours that work as hard inside the firm as outside it.

### The layered square

The enduring red square was expanded into multiple layers to speak to the countless people Maurice Blackburn supports. The layers act as backdrops that hold photography and messaging, putting clients — not the firm — at the centre of every layout. The heritage mark builds itself around the people it serves.

### Confidence without false promises

No firm can guarantee a verdict, so the brand guarantees the next best thing: expertise and care at every step. It positions Maurice Blackburn as supportive experts who see the person, not just the legal matter — confidence without the slick over-promise the category trades on.

### Fearlessly Forward

A People Commitment built from months of listening across the business — five values and five guiding behaviours that make the culture act, not just state intent. It drives recruitment, retention and how colleagues show up for clients and each other, extending the external brand inwards.

#### Signature Moment: People at the centre

### When the logo opens up to make room for the client.

The red square unfolds into layers that hold a client's face and story — the single design decision that turns a corporate mark into a stage for the people the firm exists to serve.

#### The Proof

### A brand built to extend access to justice — and to behave like it.

Maurice Blackburn now has aligned consumer-facing and employer brands, validated with real clients, equipping the firm to extend access to justice to more Australians over the next decade. Leadership described the result as "a truly wonderful strategic brand identity" and an internal brand that genuinely reflects the firm's culture.
