### Tourism Australia: Brand that behaves

#### The Behavioural Challenge

### How does a destination behave like a storyteller — when every country is shouting "visit me"?

Australia is a place like nowhere else: red dirt and eucalypt, foodies and stargazers, and the people who've been telling stories here for more than 60,000 years. In a category of postcard sameness, the stories visitors take home are the real draw. So the brand refresh for Tourism Australia couldn't behave like a glossy ad campaign — it had to behave like a storyteller, working anywhere a story is told.

#### The Designed Behaviour

### We designed the brand to behave as a storyteller, in any setting.

We put storytelling at the heart of the brand and built a system that always shows up to tell it — new colour options and a redrawn, optimised logo that hold contrast and legibility against any backdrop. Beyond a simple "g'day", the brand draws people in to discover what Australia is really about.

#### Signature Behaviours

### How the brand tells a story only this place could.

### A voice that could only be local

The brand voice welcomes you in, shares the kind of detail only a local would know, and isn't afraid to have a laugh — telling a story that could only be told here.

### Location pins with First Nations names

Pins set the scene and show exactly where stories are told — and carry the rich history of each place, with First Nations' place names appearing side by side.

### The kangaroo, embedded in the system

The iconic kangaroo's curves and form inspire the system, and a custom "Australia" wordmark hides kangaroo tails in its ink traps — recognition built into the brand's DNA, not bolted on.

### Audience stretch

The system flexes for holiday, luxury and business visitors — enriching the senses for premium audiences, inspiring business travellers — so the brand tells the right story for the reason you're here.

#### The Proof

### A national brand built to behave like the stories it tells.

Storytelling now runs through every part of Tourism Australia's brand — voice, type, place, and the kangaroo itself — giving internal teams a system that stays unmistakably Australian while flexing to whoever's listening, wherever in the world they are.
